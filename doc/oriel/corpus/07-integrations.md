## 8. Integrations

| Integration | Purpose | Who Configures | Who Uses The Results |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search Console | Search clicks, impressions, queries, pages, devices, countries | Authorized project members | Members with Analytics View |
| Google Analytics 4 | Sessions, users, channels, landing pages, countries | Authorized project members | Members with Analytics View |
| CSV Lead Import | Upload lead exports into project Leads with smart column mapping | Members who can create leads | Members who can view leads |
| WordPress Lead Capture | Push form submissions into project Leads via Lead Source Key | Members who manage lead sources | Members who can view leads |
