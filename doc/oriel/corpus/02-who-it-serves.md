## 3. Who It Serves

| Persona | Role In The Product |
|---|---|
| Agency / Platform Operator (Super Admin) | Approves projects, manages users and role templates, oversees the platform |
| Project Owner | Owns a client workspace; edits the project profile; invites the team; full operational access when the project is active |
| Project Member / Collaborator | Access based on the role assigned for that project only (for example, SEO Lead on three clients, viewer on others) |
| Client Stakeholder | Invited with view-oriented access to performance, activities, and leads |
